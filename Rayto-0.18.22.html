What's new in 0.18.22

  • Swipe shot while another menu is open — with a menu open, hold another menu's key and swipe toward a pill. The open menu closes and that pill fires, with one sound.
  • A new confirmation when a pill fires — the pill stretches like a rubber band and snaps back, lights up for a moment, leans the way you swiped, then pops like a bubble. Its border is softer and starts moving the moment the pill fires.
  • A pill fired from an open menu stays in its place — it no longer jumps outward first. A swipe shot still carries its pill out, as before.
  • A fired pill keeps the same rounded shape as every other pill, and its border follows that shape.
  • A pill you were pointing at no longer shrinks for a moment before it fires.
  • A file's preview closes cleanly — closing the preview of a file pill with ✕ no longer flashes. It now closes at once, the way the Space bar closes it.
  • No more black shapes left behind — when the Mac is busy, the pills' black shapes no longer stay on screen for a moment after a pill fires or the menu closes. A pill's shape now always moves and leaves together with its icon.
