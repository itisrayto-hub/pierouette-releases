What's new in 0.18.33

  • New: anonymous usage counts — once a day Rayto sends a few counts about how it is used: how often a menu opens and with which key, how pills are fired, whether folders, layers, dials and second actions are used, how far Setup got, and the versions of Rayto and macOS. Never pill names, apps, websites or text. It is on by default. Switch it off in Settings with Share anonymous usage counts, which also deletes what was counted on your Mac.
