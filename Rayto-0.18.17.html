What's new in 0.18.17

  • **Swipe shot: only the pill you hit shows** — the menu stays hidden until the pill fires, wherever the swipe starts, and no black circle is left at the centre. The confirmation keeps its black shape all the way under your hand.
  • **One sound when a swipe opens a folder** — the folder's own sound, without the menu's open sound on top.
  • **A smoother swipe trail** — the trail follows your display's refresh and no longer grows and shrinks under a slow hand.
