What's new in 0.18.20

  • **Window snaps land in Zen and Firefox, instantly** — Rayto now switches off the app's animated accessibility mode while it moves a window, the way Rectangle does, so a half or a corner appears at once at the size you asked for.
  • **Safeguards and options always in view** — the pill editor no longer hides them behind a "More" row.
  • **"Move into a new folder" is gone** from the pill editor — wrap a pill into a folder in the menu's folder mode instead.
  • **Links in a new window** — a link pill can open its page in a new browser window instead of a tab: on by default for a new link pill, switch it off in the pill if you want a tab. Works with Safari, Zen, Firefox, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera.
  • **No shimmer on pills with an alternative** — their faces no longer tremble while the menu opens.
  • **Problem reports that help** — Rayto now keeps a diagnostic log on your Mac by default, so a report you send carries what happened. The log never leaves your Mac by itself. Switch it off in Settings, under About.
  • **One Report a Problem** — the menu bar and Settings open the same panel: your words plus a readable diagnostic file, as an email draft you see before sending.
  • **A cleaner install window** — the disk image opens in Finder's own light or dark window, like other Mac apps, with readable labels.
