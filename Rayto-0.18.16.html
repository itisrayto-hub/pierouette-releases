What's new in 0.18.16

  • **F keys work like the modifier keys** — a quick swipe while holding your F key fires the pill as you cross it, as with ⌘ Command. A tap of an F key now opens its menu when you let go, and holding it still opens nothing.
  • **Swipe shot: the confirmation stays under your hand** — it follows the swipe and stops where your hand stops, and letting go of the key no longer cuts it short. A slightly gentler swipe now counts as a swipe shot.
  • **Held alternates survive a folder** — while you hold the menu's key for the second actions, closing a folder brings them back. The folder itself still opens on its main actions, and a folder on the second actions opens with one sound.
  • **Smoother menus** — the black shapes no longer flicker when the menu opens or a folder closes, the blur behind an open folder costs far less, and the first folder you open after a restart no longer stutters.
  • **A clearer Setup** — a page of its own for the key shot, a bigger window, animations that show the real swipe speed and start over cleanly, Continue lights up when a step is done, and the keyboard comes back to Setup after the practice menu. Setup now opens without the logo intro.
