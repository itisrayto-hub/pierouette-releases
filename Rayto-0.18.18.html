What's new in 0.18.18

  • **Swipe shot: the confirmation stops at the furthest point** — it follows your hand only while you move further from the menu; the moment you come back it stays where it was.
  • **A swipe into a folder shows the menu at once** — the blur and the menu appear together and complete, with no pills growing in.
  • **Smoother opens** — a slow first frame no longer makes the pills jump outward.
  • **First-use tips** — a tip is now a title and one short line, with a Show me button that opens its tour; the tips come at your 3rd, 10th, 20th and 30th open, and the alternates tip says to hold the menu's own key again.
  • **No desktop switch** — clicking the menu bar icon, the Shortcut Map, Parts or Report a Bug no longer moves you to another desktop.
