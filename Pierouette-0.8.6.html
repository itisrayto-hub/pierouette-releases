What's new in 0.8.6

  • Cancel now has its own voice: the red ✗, backing out of a confirmation, and closing the menu from the hub all play a soft water-drop sound, clearly distinct from firing a pill
  • Every gesture plays exactly one sound — several doubled-up cues (folder close, ⌥ flip, cancel-then-close) were removed
  • ⌥ layer flips and folder open/close now share the navigation tick
