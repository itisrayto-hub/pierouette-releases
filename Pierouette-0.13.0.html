What's new in 0.13.0

  • **New pill type: Dial** — fire it and a small knob appears at your pointer; circle around it to turn the value notch by notch. Presets: Volume, Brightness, Keyboard Light, video Seek (←/→), arrow pairs — or record any two keys for the directions. Volume/brightness/keyboard-light dials show the live level as a number while turning; arrow dials show the arrow being pressed. Hold ⌥ while circling for fine steps. Each dial pill has its own sensitivity slider in its editor. The knob grows while you turn and shrinks when you stop; it starts with a little resistance from standstill (so drifting toward the centre never changes the value), ticks the trackpad on every notch, and closes on any click — which is swallowed, so nothing underneath gets clicked
  • **New menu: the Control Deck** — a ring of eight system dials (Volume · Brightness · Keyboard Light · Seek · Track · Zoom · Tabs · Spaces). Open it from the Compass's lower diagonal seat (its pill now shows a dial and says Controls), or from the new dial button in the arrange bar. Its pills are yours to edit and arrange like any menu
  • Compass: the two invisible diagonal seats (Settings, Controls) are marked with small dots that appear and leave with the menu; the Arrange seat is retired — arrange mode is the right-click on any centre hub
  • Arrow-key dials fire into the app the menu was opened over, not whatever app was focused
  • Settings side panel: opens over the same blur layer as the menu, and the blur sliders change the frost live while you drag — the Blur slider actually works now; sliders got a small dot that snaps back to the default when you drag near it; **Sound volume** moved from the menu-bar icon into the panel
  • Deleting a pill leaves its spot empty — the ring no longer slides everything over
  • Wide glyphs (tabs, spaces, play/pause and friends) are no longer shaved at the corners
  • Hotkey pills no longer go dead until you click into another app and back — the target window is given the keyboard before every send
  • The hub keeps leaning at a folder while it opens instead of snapping upright and back
