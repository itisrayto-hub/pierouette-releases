What's new in 0.8.9

  • Folder arrange, rebuilt on the same engine as everything else: dragging, swapping, linked and unlinked pairs, and empty slots now follow exactly the same rules and animations as the main ring — live make-way, the clock-hand pair turn, and pills settling from under your cursor
  • Right-click editing works on folder pills and their outer (⌥) pills; folders show one badge per state — pencil when closed (edit the pill), gear when open (arrange the folder)
  • Editing no longer closes the menu: saving or closing an edit popup returns you to where you were — including Arrange mode, which also got a visible ✕ and click-outside-to-close
  • Slot numbers in the folder arrange, matching the main ring
  • Sub-menu layout is identical in Arrange and live use (pills no longer shift when entering arrange)
  • The folder's name and look now appear in the center hub of its menu (it was empty)
  • Accidental fires fixed: leftover swipe momentum after a Compass switch can't trigger a pill, and closing an edit popup can't fire one on your next mouse move
  • The auto-recenter sound follows the flight's length and loudness; the flight speed itself is unchanged
