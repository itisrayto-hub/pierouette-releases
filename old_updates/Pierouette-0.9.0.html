What's new in 0.9.0

  • Folder arrange is finished: every combination of what you hold and where you aim now does one defined thing. Mains swap with mains, a linked couple trades its whole slice, an unlinked pair moves one half at a time, and dragging a pill onto its own outer seat swaps the two sides of that slice
  • The fan waits for you to stop. While your hand is still travelling across the ring nothing rearranges — it reacts when you pause, and it holds the picture it is showing instead of snapping back the moment you leave a seat
  • Releasing is always instant: drop a pill before the pause elapses and it lands immediately, exactly where the blue target said
  • The grey link marks stay with the pair they belong to: they no longer vanish mid-drag, follow the wrong pill, or slide across the ring when two slices trade
  • A folder can grow at either end — drag a pill past the first or the last slice and a new slice opens there. A linked couple moves to it whole and can never be split by a drag
  • Drag from the Pill Library straight into a folder you are arranging
  • Undo and redo reach every arrange gesture, and the undo bar sits clear of the outer pills
  • Main ring: swap is the default everywhere — hold Option to shift the whole row instead. The outer pills, the empty outer seats and the link marks all travel on the same glide as the mains, so a whole slice moves as one piece
  • Switching modes with a swipe now replaces the ring in the direction you swiped, and every menu you land in announces itself with the same sound
  • The App Launcher's icon-only look now applies to its outer pills too, and a folder's look carries to every face inside it
  • Dictation works while the menu is up — the menu hands the keyboard over instead of swallowing it
  • Faster drops: saving your arrangement no longer stalls the release (about 120ms off every drop), and hovering costs nothing while the target is unchanged
