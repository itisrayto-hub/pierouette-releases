What's new in 0.12.0

  • The pill library learned your habits: the Suggestions tile (formerly "Clipboard") now also lists the shortcuts you actually press in the app that was in front when you opened the menu — newest first, named from the app's own menu bar with the keys beneath. Drag one onto a ring and it becomes a Switch + Shortcut pill for that app. Reflexes like ⌘C/⌘V, your own triggers and shortcuts already on a ring are left out
  • The Tray: collect pills while arranging and drop the copies into another menu. Opening the library always lands on the categories; pull down for the A-Z list, and the pill you drag rides above the library on its way out
  • Escape now backs out exactly one step: an open confirm, then a folder, then a sub-menu, then the menu — and inside an editor window it closes that window. Closing an editor with unsaved edits asks Save / Discard / Cancel; Cancel on an edit page really discards, the ring snaps back
  • The ✓/✕ confirm beside a pill inside a folder emerges from under its pill and retracts into it at the folder's own pace; it always sits above its neighbours, and backing out of it sounds like navigation, not a refusal
  • Editor windows open beside the ring — right of it, left if there's no room — and every page of the editor re-places itself there; the pointer no longer jumps when an editor opens or after cancelling a confirm
  • The little name/shortcut bubble on hover now sits on the line from the centre through the pill, for every pill
  • The "3D light" rim around the hub and pills is gone for good
  • Compass: the hub no longer leans toward the pointer; ↑/↓ select a pill, ←/→ switch between the universal menus; holding a navigation key no longer machine-guns; the menu closes with the same sound it opened with, and cancelling keeps its own sound
  • The pointer trail shows only while you're using the menu — never in arrange mode or under an editor
  • Right-clicking the hub: the gear now gives the same feedback as the pencil on a pill
  • Folder feel: a folder you close by pulling back stays closed until you step off the pill and come back; the goo strand no longer sticks out after a folder closes, and it stops short of coloured pills
  • Safeguards in the editor sit 2×2 with the undo seconds on their own row; the undo cancel target aims where the pill actually flew, inside folders too
  • Smoother openings: the menu is fully drawn before it appears (no half-drawn frames after long idles or on the first open after wake), and swapping between menus keeps the pills together with their bodies
