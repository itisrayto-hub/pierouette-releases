What's new in 0.10.1

  • A folder no longer opens and closes rapidly when your hand rests where it opened — pulling back toward the center still closes it, but only an actual pull does; a still hand never closes a folder
  • The pill you grab in a folder's arrange mode now wiggles as smoothly as its neighbors
  • A grabbed alternative (the ⌥ face) keeps wiggling in your hand instead of freezing — on both the menu and inside folders
