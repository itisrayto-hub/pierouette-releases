What's new in 0.17.0

  • **Licence keys** — Settings › About › Licence › Manage…: click the box to paste your key. Keys are checked on your Mac, no account, no internet needed. Get one at rayto.app or from Ray
  • **Alpha builds expire** — an alpha stops 60 days after it was built and asks you to update; the latest version keeps everything you set up
  • **One permission flow** — Rayto explains Accessibility first, then macOS asks; the two no longer appear at the same time
  • **Reinstall no longer reports a crash** — the false "quit unexpectedly" box after reinstalling is gone
