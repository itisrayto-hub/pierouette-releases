What's new in 0.8.10

  • Arrange the Compass itself: right-click its hub → gear, then drag the four menu portals to trade places — your layout decides which arrow key and which swipe direction opens which menu, and it sticks
  • New: copy a link, open the menu, enter arrange — a ready-made pill appears at the screen's top-right corner. Notion links become real Notion pills (they open as a new tab in the Notion app); other links become website pills. Drag it into a slot, onto a folder, or onto an occupied pill (the occupant moves to its alternative spot)
  • Right-click editing now reaches the outer (alternative) pills in folder menus
  • Fresh installs start with swipe-down opening the app-specific Context menu (Window moves to the right)
