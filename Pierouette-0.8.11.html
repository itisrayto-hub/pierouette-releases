What's new in 0.8.11

  • Folder arrange, finished: dragging a pill now shows exactly where it will land — one blue target for a single pill, two for a linked couple (it takes both seats) — and the pills that have to move step aside while you drag, so releasing changes nothing you haven't already seen
  • Linked couples travel as one slice and can't be split by a drag or have half of them evicted; open (unlinked) pairs move independently — drag a main onto a main and only the mains swap
  • Both layers now animate identically: outer (⌥) pills travel on the same glide as the mains instead of springing into place
  • Undo and redo in arrange mode: ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z, plus buttons above the ring
  • Deleting a pill in arrange asks first, and deleted pills are kept in the Pill Library's History
  • Pill Library at the top-right of arrange mode: your saved pills, deleted pills, and a suggestion made from whatever link you last copied — drag any of them straight into a slot
  • Edits apply immediately: changing a pill's safeguard, label or colour now takes effect without closing and reopening the menu
  • A pad tap can't switch menus while you're dragging in arrange
  • The menu-bar volume slider uses the full width of the menu
