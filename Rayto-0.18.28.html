What's new in 0.18.28

  • App volume explains itself — rest the pointer on an App volume dial and a short card says what it does: it turns the volume of the app under the pointer. The card shows three times at most and stops once you turned the dial.
  • New installs — the starter menu has an App volume dial beside Volume.
  • A blocked App volume dial says so — when macOS has not allowed Rayto to change an app's volume, the dial shows a red crossed speaker and does not turn. A card with Open Settings takes you to the switch. An app no longer goes silent without a word.
