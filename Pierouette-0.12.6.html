What's new in 0.12.6

  • Compass: two new circles flank the universal menu — **Arrange** (between the app launcher and the universal menu) and **Settings** (between the universal menu and the window menu). They stay invisible until you lean toward them and need a little more travel than a menu swap: Arrange opens the Compass itself in arrange mode, Settings closes the menu and opens the settings side panel (or closes it if it is already open)
  • Compass arrange mode: reorder the four menus on the top/right/bottom/left seats and move Arrange/Settings among the diagonal seats; the bar has undo/redo, the four menu buttons and a separate Compass button; clicking a menu button opens that menu's arrange mode with the Pill Library
  • Right-click any centre hub — main menu, Compass or an open folder — to enter arrange mode directly (no gear step)
  • Compass: the context and window menus still answer when there is no window under the cursor — the pill swaps in, springs back, and you get the cancel sound plus the "No window under the cursor" message
  • Compass aiming is calmer: nothing reacts until your hand is past the inner half of the hub, small wobbles no longer re-aim at a neighbour, aiming no longer locks into the first direction you moved, and the hub follows your hand with weight instead of one-to-one
  • Settings side panel: opens on the display you are working on, opens without a hitch from the Compass, hides when you enter arrange mode, and a shortcut recording is cancelled the moment the panel closes
  • The first open after launching the app (or after an update) is already warm — no more cold stutter on the first menu
  • Four-finger tap: a fresh hand landing on the pad always counts, even right after another gesture; a rare crash in the trackpad monitor is fixed
  • Pill Library: **Layouts** — save named snapshots of a menu as memory cards and restore them; the library orders itself around the menu you are arranging and its app; the badge on a card now means "on THIS menu" (check) or "lives on another menu" (arrow, named on hover) and tapping the check flashes the pill's slot; your own fired shortcuts no longer count toward Most Used
  • The fixed 8-slot layout is now on by default for every app's context menu
  • A tinted open folder tilts as one object toward your pointer
  • The goo strand meets every pill the same way on every ring — straight along the centre line, a slim neck into the pill, the same width at the junction everywhere — and hovering a pill swells it without distorting its neighbours
  • Confirm pairs: only the check survives the commit (the ✕ leaves with it), inside folders the same as on the main ring; the folder hub ticks like the main hub
  • Arrange: the minus shows only on the pill under your pointer, the open folder no longer wiggles, and the strand from the hub to an open folder stays while you arrange; the slot-count badge on folder pills is gone
