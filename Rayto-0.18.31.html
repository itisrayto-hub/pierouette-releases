What's new in 0.18.31

  • New: only with a finger on the trackpad — a switch in Settings, in the card Single keys. When it is on, a single key opens its menu only while a finger rests on the trackpad, so a stray tap while you type opens nothing. Swipe shot and key shot work as before. It is off until you turn it on.
  • A key shot at a folder looks clean — tap the key and press a direction toward a folder: the menu now appears complete and the folder opens on it. Before, the menu and the folder grew at the same moment.
