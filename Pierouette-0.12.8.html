What's new in 0.12.8

  • New pill options under **Switch**: Context · Shortcut · **System** — switch to an app and press one of the keyboard's own keys (Play/Pause, Next/Previous, Volume, Mute, Brightness, Keyboard Light, Apps, Mission Control)
  • The same keys are also available as plain **System** pills, without switching apps
  • The universal menu always opens on its **first** mode; hold ⌥ while opening (or while swiping to the Universal pill on the Compass — the pill flips to its second face) for the second mode
  • New in Settings → Feel: **Return pointer after firing** — after a pill fires, the pointer glides back to where you opened the menu (off by default)
  • Compass: after an unavailable menu springs back, nothing reacts until your hand is back at the centre — no accidental re-swaps on the way home
  • A folder closed by clicking it no longer reopens when you move back toward the centre — a pill only ever triggers on an inside-to-outside motion
  • Window pills act on the window you hovered only when it really is that window; a window opened a moment earlier no longer loses the command to an older one
  • Much smoother under the hand: everything that can appear during a gesture (toasts, the pill library, the arm indicator, the shortcut map, both settings surfaces) is pre-built at launch; the first open after launching — including the Compass — is pre-warmed; several background reads left the main thread
