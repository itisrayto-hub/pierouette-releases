What's new in 0.12.7

  • Swipe open a folder and the pointer glides to the folder's centre — the same flight as after a Compass swap (clicking a folder open stays put)
  • The first menu open after launching or updating the app is warm — no cold stutter any more
  • Arrange mode: dropping a linked couple by its ⌥ lands it where you release it (it used to jump back to its old seat and slide over); dragging an ⌥ in a folder makes one sound, not two; no empty dashed box appears at the seat you are dragging a couple away from
  • Over the bare desktop, the hub shows Finder, and the arrange bar's context button arranges Finder's context menu (it showed the front app's pills under a Finder icon); Context/Window stay unavailable over the desktop as before
  • Compass aiming: nothing reacts until your hand is past the inner half of the hub, and tiny wobbles no longer re-aim at a neighbour
