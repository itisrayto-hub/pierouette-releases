What's new in 0.12.4

  • Pill library: the search list shows one card per pill — the pill's face, its name, what it does (e.g. "Shortcut · ⌘K", "App · Notion", "Link · notion.so"), and where it lives ("Play · slot 3", "DaVinci Resolve · Context menu · slot 6", "Not on any menu"); each pill appears once (no duplicates across History / Saved / Menus)
  • Pill library: pull down on the categories to reveal the search list — a short pull springs back, a deliberate pull commits; Cancel fades back to the categories; the library is a full-height column at the screen edge with Apple-glass category cards
  • Arrange mode: switch between your menus without leaving arrange — four menu buttons on the bar (left of undo) wearing the Compass pills' faces; the hub no longer shows an ✕ in arrange mode
  • New pill type: System → **Arrange Mode** — a pill that puts the open menu into arrange mode (obeys the click-only / hard-press safeguards like any pill)
  • Settings: the menu-bar "Settings…" now opens a compact side panel with the everyday settings (Menu · Blur · Sounds · General); "All Settings…" opens the full window
  • Compass: a lane that isn't available right now dims only its glyph — the pill body stays; a switch that is refused in the opening beat does nothing instead of opening the launcher as a folder
  • Fixed: the goo strand could stay attached after resting on a pill and moving back to the centre
  • Fixed: the pill library vanished behind the blur when a screenshot shortcut (e.g. Shottr's ⇧⌘2) was pressed during arrange
