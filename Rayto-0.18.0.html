What's new in 0.18.0

  • **Design the four menus** — click a menu's pill on the Compass to pick its glyph, pill colour and glyph colour; the centre hub and every place that shows the menu follow. "Back to how the app ships" resets it
  • **One colour picker everywhere** — presets, your saved colours, a hex field with a live preview, and the macOS colour wheel with its eyedropper
  • **Window pills tell the truth** — an app's windows are counted from its real window list, closed chats no longer leave a ghost pill, a browser tab's pill takes the page's title, and the counts update the moment the ring opens
  • **Make a menu for this app** — Rayto reads an app's commands and builds a menu from the best of them
  • **Arrange: the Library column is always the wall** — the ring stays put when you switch menus from the side panel
  • **A fast swing back through the centre closes a folder** — it used to stay open if you were quicker than a quarter second
  • **The centre hub keeps its ⌥ face while the menu closes** — no flicker back to the plain face
  • **Undo countdowns always finish** — quitting a window through the undo ring works even if you open the menu during the countdown
  • **W and S turn a floating dial** — like the arrow keys
  • **Lifetime licence** — one key, every future version
  • **Acknowledgements** — the open-source components and their licences, in the About card
