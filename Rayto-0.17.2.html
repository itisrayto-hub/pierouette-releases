What's new in 0.17.2

  • **One Trigger setting** — Settings › Trigger is now a ladder of numbered levels: Click, Swipe past the pill, Swipe toward the pill (expert). It replaces the two separate switches
  • **Setup has a third lesson** — tap ⌘, then use the arrow keys to pick a pill
  • **Smoother Arrange** — entering and leaving Arrange, picking a pill up and the folder bloom no longer stutter
  • **Dials: fine steps are ⌥⇧, like macOS** — hold Option+Shift (either Option) while turning a dial for small steps, the keys macOS uses on its volume and brightness keys. Option alone no longer makes steps finer
  • **Hyper + a modifier + the arrow keys use the menu's own arrows** — diagonals work, the pill fires when you let go, and you see its confirmation
