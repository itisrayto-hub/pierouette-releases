What's new in 0.18.15

  • **Function keys** — open any menu with an F key, for when your hand sits higher: its own card in Settings, one key per menu. "Mirror on the right" makes F12 to F9 open what F1 to F4 open, for the other hand. The card tells you what your Mac's top row does and where the switch for it is.
  • **⌥ Option is a menu key like any other** — the dedicated alternates key is gone. The second actions come by tapping the menu's own key again and holding, by a double click on the centre, or by the twist. A double tap's second press shows them at once; a hold shows them after a quarter second.
  • **Replace by dropping** — drop a pill on a taken seat and it replaces at once; the card offers Undo, which puts the occupant back and the newcomer in your hand again. A full folder says so instead of taking the pill.
  • **Suggestion cards for links, files and shortcuts** — a copied link shows the page's title and icon; a copied file or folder, or a path on the clipboard, is offered as a pill; an app's own shortcut you press on two days gets a card with its keys as key caps.
  • **The alternates flip no longer lags** — switching to the second actions and back redraws nothing it does not need to.
