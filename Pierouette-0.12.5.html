What's new in 0.12.5

  • Four-finger tap, rebuilt on the trackpad's own sensor states: a palm resting on the pad with a three-finger tap or drag can never open the Compass any more, while real four-finger taps (including beside a resting palm, or landing four and clicking later) fire reliably; taps right after the Mac wakes no longer misfire
  • Press W / A / S / D while any menu is open to jump straight to that Compass menu (e.g. A in the universal menu opens the app launcher); if your navigation keys are set to WASD they keep navigating
  • A folder's ring of pills now matches the main ring's size exactly
  • Pills inside a folder never stick to or merge with a close neighbour any more — at rest, while hovering, and while the folder opens
  • Inside a folder, the strand now meets each pill centred on the side facing the folder — no more off-centre attachment in rearranged folders
  • Hovering any pill now swells the whole pill — body and icon together, the same motion as a folder charging
  • Firing a pill no longer bounces it — the pill holds still and the ring pulse is the confirmation
  • An open folder tilts as a whole toward your pointer again, and while a folder is open the menu's centre stays put — only the folder follows your hand; a Confirm/Cancel pair keeps the centre on your cursor
  • The hub bounces softly when a menu swap lands, and its glyph changes in a clean cut
  • The goo strand now meets coloured pills exactly like black ones, keeps a clean neck through the ⌥ flip instead of jittering, and flows off the hub's edge like off a folder pill
  • Faster and steadier: the first open after a long idle no longer stutters, and switching menus in the Compass keeps the pills alive instead of rebuilding them (the swap-glide hitch is gone)
  • Settings side panel: the cards wear the same clean glass as the pill library, tuned to stay colour-true
