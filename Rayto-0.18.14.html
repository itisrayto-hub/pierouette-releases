What's new in 0.18.14

  • **Key shot** — tap the menu key and press a direction key at once: the pill fires and its confirmation appears black under the pointer. A direction key on an open menu fires as before
  • **Settings, one card per way** — the ⌘ Command tab lists each way to open as its own card; the hand tab has "Swipe shot" and "Key shot", each with a switch and its settings
  • **App keys, set where you see them** — in arrange on an app's menu, a key cap under each layer number records the modifier that opens that layer in that app, with a clear badge; the old global switch is gone
  • **Library suggestions** — a suggestion row shows the pill's name, then its kind and what it does
