What's new in 0.18.12

  • **Starter menus** — a fresh install now comes set up: the main menu's second layer (press 2) holds snippets, a copy-to-Notes macro, New Note, Calculator, Downloads, Wi-Fi, Do Not Disturb and Empty Trash; the Clipboard folder gains Emoji
  • **App menus out of the box** — a ⌥ Option tap in Safari, Finder, Mail, Notes, Messages, Music or Preview opens that app's eight most-used commands, with the app's own keys
